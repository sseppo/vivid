# Product brief — Vivid (name not settled)

> Pointer and one paragraph, never a new product document (cross-project-mechanics §11). First text

**Read the product in:** `docs/context/sources/01-master-vision.md` (current edition) **and**
`docs/context/sources/01-master-vision-fullest-edition.md` (fuller earlier edition — later revisions lost
content to Gem context limits; D-5). The rest of the master series is beside them in `docs/context/sources/`

architecture in `docs/architecture/`; dashboards in `docs/design/dashboards/` (D-4).

**In one paragraph, as those files state it:** Vivid is planned as a hybrid marketplace that sells
outcomes of AI image and video generation — "memories, stories and heirlooms" rather than a generation
tool: cheap instant digital products, premium physical products made by partners, and a manual service
tier delivered by Content Creator Agents (CCA). A *Curator* runs a family project and its vault,
*Contributors* are invited to add assets and chip in credits, and *Partners* are IP holders or
manufacturers. *Sagas* are sequential multi-step product lines in which each step unlocks the next and
inherits the earlier steps' context; *Collections* are unordered sets. Generation requests are routed by
cost and content through three lanes — public APIs, a self-hosted "Sanctuary Server" with its own
classifier, and a hard block.

**Unsettled, and not decided here:** the product name (D-7, X-4); the credit peg is the latest definition, provisionally (1 credit =
€0.10, D-13); backend shape, ORM and deployment target (X-6, X-7, X-8).
