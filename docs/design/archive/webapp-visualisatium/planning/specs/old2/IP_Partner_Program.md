# BUSINESS LOGIC: THE IP PARTNER PROGRAM ("Official Worlds")
**Context:** User wants "Star Wars". We can't give it. But we can give "Galactic Knights" OR "Official Indie Worlds".

## 1. TIER 1: THE "LEGALLY DISTINCT" THEMES (Internal)
Managed by Visualisatium.
* **"The Galaxy Wars"** (Not Star Wars)
* **"The Wizarding Academy"** (Not Harry Potter)
* **"The Wasteland Wanderer"** (Not Fallout)
* **Asset:** We train generic LoRAs that capture the *vibe* without infringing trademarks.

## 2. TIER 2: THE "OFFICIAL PARTNER" PORTAL (External)
**Target:** Indie Authors, Comic Creators, Game Studios, Influencers.
* **The Pitch:** "Let fans put themselves in YOUR world."
* **The Mechanism:**
    * Creator uploads their **Official LoRA** (Art Style + Characters).
    * Creator uploads their **Lore Bible** (World Rules).
* **The Product:** "Official [Book Name] Character Creator".

### The Revenue Split
* **Price:** User pays **Standard Price + 20% Premium** (e.g., €6 instead of €5).
* **Split:** The 20% Premium goes *directly* to the IP Holder.
* **Benefit:**
    * **For Us:** Exclusive content, free marketing (Creator tells their fans).
    * **For Creator:** Passive income, fan engagement.

* **Location:** In the "Theme Selector" (Split Choice UI).
* **Text:** "Don't see your favorite world? Request it!"
