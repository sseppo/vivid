# Operational Manual & UX Vision

## 1. SUPPORT & COMMUNICATION
*   **Structure:** Chat is ALWAYS tied to a "Subject" (Order ID or Ticket ID).
*   **Multi-Party:** Chats support `User` + `Admin` + `Partner` (Tri-party) if physical goods are involved.
*   **Persistence:** Chat history is never deleted; parties can "Revive" a closed thread.
*   **Wishlist:** A "Feature Request" area located within the Support Dashboard. Users upvote ideas for Phase 3 Inventory expansion.

## 2. NAVIGATION PHILOSOPHY
*   **Dual-Entry:** Users can browse by `Product` (Image) OR `Category` (Memories).
*   **"Show Me Everything":** Even when selecting a Category, products are visible immediately to reduce clicks.
*   **Fast-Track Input:** Users can skip "Selection Lists" via a prominent "I know what I want" button (Hybrid Input).

## 3. PHYSICAL GOODS PARTNERSHIP
*   **Role:** We are the "Middle Man" but NOT the manufacturer.
*   **Liability:** Terms must explicitly exclude liability for Partner printing errors.
*   **Flow:** "Information Products" (selling a Partner's contact info) are a distinct SKU type.
